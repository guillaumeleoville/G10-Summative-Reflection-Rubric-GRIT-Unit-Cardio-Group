How to use this rubric

  • Read each level carefully before giving yourself a score — be honest, not generous.
  • Your score must be backed by a specific example from a lesson. "I tried hard" is not evidence. "In Lesson 4, during the partner burpee drill, I kept going even though I wanted to stop" is evidence.
  • Your teacher will compare your self-assessment to theirs. If they're very different, you'll discuss why.
